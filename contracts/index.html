    Smart contracts¶

    The shop's limits live on chain, in a contract the agent cannot change. These are the contracts in contracts/ (Solidity 0.8.37, Arc Foundry).

    Deployed on Arc Testnet¶

    Every embco contract below, and every shop contract that exists today, has its source verified (exact match) on Sourcify and in the explorer. The vaults and USDC are not embco's. A contract an owner creates later is verified the same way, with the source in this repository:

    arc-forge verify-contract <address> src/ShopPayables.sol:ShopPayables --chain-id 5042002 --verifier sourcify --watch

    Use ShopEarn or ShopFunding (with the chain's id) for the other two. The explorer picks up what Sourcify verified.

    Who can do what¶

    The owner can also call pay, and the limits do not apply to the owner. Every other caller is refused with NotAuthorized.

    How a payment is accepted¶

    Each invoiceRef is a unique reference for the invoice (the contract documents a hash of its ERP name as one example), so it can be paid once and matched to its invoice. The transfer uses transferFrom: the money stays in the owner's wallet until the call succeeds, and revoking the USDC allowance stops every payment.

    Limits¶

    • maxPerPayment is greater than zero and no more than weeklyCap.
    • The weekly window starts when the shop is created and runs in seven-day blocks.
    • Only the owner changes limits. Every change emits LimitsChanged.

    Events¶

    Event Emitted when
    Paid An invoice is paid, with its reference, payee, amount and caller
    LimitsChanged The owner changes the per-payment or weekly limit
    AgentChanged The owner sets the agent's address
    PayeeSet The owner approves or removes a supplier wallet
    Paused / Unpaused The agent or owner pauses; only the owner resumes

    The dashboard reads these events from the chain to show suppliers and payments.

    ShopEarn: idle USDC in vaults¶

    Each owner creates one ShopEarn from ShopEarnFactory, approves the vaults the agent may use and sets a per-park limit and a weekly cap.

    Who Can do Cannot do
    Agent park: move the owner's USDC into an approved vault, within the per-park limit and the weekly cap; unpark / unparkAll: bring it back to the owner's wallet at any time; pause Use a vault the owner did not approve, change a limit, send USDC or shares anywhere but the owner
    Owner Everything the agent can, with no limit; approve vaults, set the limits and the agent; resume Transfer ownership

    Neither USDC nor vault shares stay in ShopEarn: park deposits with the owner as the receiver, so the shares go to the owner's wallet, and unpark redeems them back to the owner. Taking money back does not use the weekly cap, so the agent can always recall funds to pay a bill. Every move emits Parked or Unparked, which the dashboard reads for its Earn card.

    JournalAnchor: the journal, checkable by anyone¶

    After money moves, and at least once a day, the agent writes the Merkle root (RFC 9162) of its whole journal and how many entries it covers.

    Who Can do Cannot do
    The agent the shop contract names anchor(shop, root, size), with a size larger than the last one Change or remove an anchor, anchor a smaller journal
    Anyone Read latest, count and anchorAt for any shop Anchor for a shop that does not name them as its agent

    The contract has no owner. embco verify recomputes the root from a copy of the journal and compares it with every anchor: a changed, removed or reordered entry gives a different root.

    DecisionVerifier: one decision, checked by Arc¶

    Who Can do Cannot do
    Anyone verify(proof): a decision's MANIFEST entry, its audit path and its anchor, as one value Change anything: it has no owner, no state and no write function

    verify hashes the entry as the journal does, follows the RFC 9162 audit path and compares the root with the anchor the shop's agent wrote in JournalAnchor. Only if it matches does it read the decision out of the entry and return it, with the shop's owner(). The entry is read at fixed places, never as general JSON, and free text arrives as hex, so a record of any other shape is refused. See Audit the agent.

    ShopFunding: the owner's USDC on other chains¶

    Chain ShopFundingFactory
    Base Sepolia 0x69e31Af302Ee222204AA78099D39a2956F5C95d6
    Ethereum Sepolia 0x69e31Af302Ee222204AA78099D39a2956F5C95d6

    Both factories are verified on Sourcify for each chain (Base Sepolia, Ethereum Sepolia).

    ShopFunding is the same idea on a source chain of the owner's Unified Balance (Circle Gateway). Each owner creates one from ShopFundingFactory on that chain and approves it, the way they approve the shop contract on Arc.

    Who Can do Cannot do
    Agent fund(amount): take that much of the owner's USDC and deposit it into the owner's own Gateway balance, within a per-move and a weekly cap; pause Send it anywhere else, change a limit, resume after a pause
    Owner Everything the agent can, with no limit; set the limits and the agent; resume Transfer ownership

    The money never stays in the contract: fund takes it from the owner's wallet and calls Gateway's depositFor(usdc, owner, amount) in the same transaction, so the deposit always belongs to the owner. From there the agent, which the owner authorizes as a delegate in Gateway, brings it to the owner's wallet on Arc. Unit and fuzz tests, plus a fork test against Base Sepolia's real GatewayWallet and USDC.
