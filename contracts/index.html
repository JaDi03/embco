    Smart contracts¶

    The shop's limits live on chain, in a contract the agent cannot change. These are the contracts in contracts/ (Solidity 0.8.37, Arc Foundry).

    Who can do what¶

    The owner can also call pay, and the limits do not apply to the owner. Every other caller is refused with NotAuthorized.

    How a payment is accepted¶

    Each invoiceRef is a unique reference for the invoice (the contract documents a hash of its ERP name as one example), so it can be paid once and matched to its invoice. The transfer uses transferFrom: the money stays in the owner's wallet until the call succeeds, and revoking the USDC allowance stops every payment.

    Limits¶

    • maxPerPayment is greater than zero and no more than weeklyCap.
    • The weekly window starts when the shop is created and runs in seven-day blocks.
    • Only the owner changes limits. Every change emits LimitsChanged.

    Events¶

    Event Emitted when
    Paid An invoice is paid, with its reference, payee, amount and caller
    LimitsChanged The owner changes the per-payment or weekly limit
    AgentChanged The owner sets the agent's address
    PayeeSet The owner approves or removes a supplier wallet
    Paused / Unpaused The agent or owner pauses; only the owner resumes

    The dashboard reads these events from the chain to show suppliers and payments.

    ShopFunding: the owner's USDC on other chains¶

    Chain ShopFundingFactory
    Base Sepolia 0x69e31Af302Ee222204AA78099D39a2956F5C95d6
    Ethereum Sepolia 0x69e31Af302Ee222204AA78099D39a2956F5C95d6

    ShopFunding is the same idea on a source chain of the owner's Unified Balance (Circle Gateway). Each owner creates one from ShopFundingFactory on that chain and approves it, the way they approve the shop contract on Arc.

    Who Can do Cannot do
    Agent fund(amount): take that much of the owner's USDC and deposit it into the owner's own Gateway balance, within a per-move and a weekly cap; pause Send it anywhere else, change a limit, resume after a pause
    Owner Everything the agent can, with no limit; set the limits and the agent; resume Transfer ownership

    The money never stays in the contract: fund takes it from the owner's wallet and calls Gateway's depositFor(usdc, owner, amount) in the same transaction, so the deposit always belongs to the owner. From there the agent, which the owner authorizes as a delegate in Gateway, brings it to the owner's wallet on Arc. Unit and fuzz tests, plus a fork test against Base Sepolia's real GatewayWallet and USDC.
