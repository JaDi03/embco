    Connect your ERPNext¶

    This guide is for the person who runs the shop's ERPNext. You will make four small changes in ERPNext and then paste two keys into the embco dashboard. Nothing is installed on your ERPNext, and the agent never gets admin rights.

    Before you start

    • Your shop is already created in the dashboard: your wallet is connected and your shop contract exists (create it in Settings). Creating it asks for one free signature and one transaction, and the shop comes with its own agent: you never set it up or send it gas.
    • You can sign in to ERPNext as an administrator (System Manager) to make the changes below.
    • Your ERPNext opens with an https:// address. embco is tested with ERPNext v16, including Frappe Cloud.

    Step 1: Create a user for the agent¶

    The agent signs in to ERPNext as its own user, with only the roles it needs.

    1. In ERPNext, create a new User for the agent (for example agent@your-shop.com).
    2. Give it these three roles, and no others:
      • Accounts User
      • Purchase User
      • Stock User
    3. Open that user and, under API Access, generate its keys. ERPNext shows an API Key and an API Secret. Copy both now: the secret is shown only once.

    With these roles the agent can read suppliers, purchase orders, receipts and invoices. The only thing it writes is the Payment Entry for an invoice it paid.

    Step 2: Require an order and a receipt for every invoice¶

    This is what lets the agent check that what you were billed is what you ordered and received.

    1. Open Buying Settings.
    2. Set the option that requires a Purchase Order to Yes.
    3. Set the option that requires a Purchase Receipt to Yes.
    4. Save.

    Both are No in a new ERPNext.

    Step 3: Make room for wallet addresses¶

    A wallet address is 42 characters long, and ERPNext's bank account number field holds 30. You change this once:

    1. Open Customize Form and choose Bank Account.
    2. Find the field Bank Account No and set its Length to 64.
    3. Save.

    Step 4: Add your suppliers' wallets¶

    Each supplier's wallet is saved as a bank account at a bank called USDC on Arc. The supplier's real bank account stays as it is.

    1. Create a Bank named USDC on Arc (once).
    2. For each supplier you will pay with embco, create a Bank Account:
      • Bank: USDC on Arc
      • Party Type: Supplier
      • Party: the supplier
      • Bank Account No: the supplier's wallet address (starts with 0x)

    One wallet per supplier

    If a supplier has two active accounts at USDC on Arc with different wallets, the agent holds its invoices and does not choose between them.

    A new or changed wallet is never paid right away. The supplier first confirms it is theirs, and then you approve it in the dashboard (see What happens next).

    Step 5: Connect in the dashboard¶

    1. Open app.embco.xyz and Sign in with your wallet, if you did not when you created the shop. It is a signature, not a transaction: it costs nothing.

    2. In Your ERPNext, fill in:

      Field What to write
      ERPNext address The address you open ERPNext with, for example https://your-shop.frappe.cloud
      Company Your company's name exactly as it is written in ERPNext
      API key The key from Step 1
      API secret The secret from Step 1
      Bank that holds supplier wallets USDC on Arc (already filled in)
      Extra payment fields Leave it empty, unless your ERPNext asks for extra fields when you submit a payment by hand (some local tax setups do). Then ask whoever set up your ERPNext which fields and values to write

    3. Click Test and connect. The dashboard tries the keys without changing anything in ERPNext, and shows each check:

      Check What it means
      Keys ERPNext accepted the agent's key (required)
      Invoices The agent can read your unpaid purchase invoices (required)
      Company The company name was found
      Payments ERPNext can draft a payment for an invoice (nothing is saved)
      Wallets How many supplier wallets were found at USDC on Arc

    4. A shop created before its agent came with it shows Make it the agent: click it once. This lets your shop's agent wallet pay approved suppliers, within the limits you set.

    What happens next¶

    • The agent checks ERPNext on its own every 15 minutes. You do not need to open anything.
    • A new supplier wallet is confirmed by the supplier at app.embco.xyz/supplier, with a signature from that wallet. Then it appears in the dashboard under Waiting for your approval. Before approving, check in ERPNext that the wallet belongs to the supplier you expect.
    • When the agent pays, it records a Payment Entry in ERPNext for that invoice. The transaction hash is in Reference No, and the wallet paid is in the remarks.
    • Other chains the agent can pay from (optional): in Settings > Which chains can the agent pay from?, click Choose chains, turn on the chains where you keep USDC (Base Sepolia, Ethereum Sepolia) and click Save. Your wallet asks once for each chain that changed when it batches calls (MetaMask with its smart account, EIP-5792), otherwise once per step, and it needs that chain's gas token (test ETH). Turning a chain on creates your funding contract there with your shop's limits, approves it, and authorizes the agent in your Unified Balance (Circle Gateway). Your USDC stays in your wallet there. When an invoice needs more than your wallet on Arc holds, the agent moves it into your Unified Balance and brings it to your wallet on Arc, choosing which chains it comes from. It never sends it anywhere else, and your limits still apply. The agent needs no gas: its network fees are paid by Circle Gas Station. After you change your shop's limits, a chain shows "save to use your shop's limits": click Save. Turning a chain off removes the approval and the agent's authorization there. What is already in your Unified Balance stays yours.
    • Telegram (optional): in the dashboard, Connect Telegram gives a code to send to the embco bot. Then /status says how the agent is, /pause pauses payments while the agent keeps watching, and /stop turns the agent off and pauses the shop contract. Only the dashboard turns it back on. Any other message goes to your agent.

    Draft mode¶

    While embco runs on testnet, payments are made in test USDC. So that test USDC never closes a real invoice, each Payment Entry stays as a draft whose remarks start with "TESTNET". This is draft mode, and it is on for every new shop.

    • The dashboard shows the mode next to the agent's status: Draft mode or Submitting to ERPNext.
    • To change it, open Settings > ERPNext. Turning draft mode off asks you to confirm a second time: from then on each payment is submitted and closes its invoice, even when it was paid in test USDC, and your books show those invoices as paid.
    • Turning it back on takes one click. Each change is written in Your agent, and if the agent is on, it restarts with the new mode.

    If something does not work¶

    Problem What to do
    Keys check fails Check the address starts with https://, and generate the keys again (Step 1)
    Invoices check fails Check the agent's user has the three roles from Step 1
    Payments check fails Try to submit a payment to a supplier by hand in ERPNext. If you cannot either, an app or a required field is blocking it (seen with a Mexican e-invoicing app). Fix that in ERPNext first, or use Extra payment fields
    Wallets check finds 0 Check the Bank is named exactly USDC on Arc and each Bank Account has Party Type Supplier
