How to sign, step by step
  1. Install MetaMask on your computer, or open this page inside the MetaMask app on your phone.
  2. In MetaMask, choose the wallet you are paid to.
  3. Press Sign in with your wallet. MetaMask asks to add or switch to the Arc test network: accept.
  4. MetaMask shows a sign-in message with your wallet. Read it and press Sign.
  5. If a shop asks you to confirm your wallet, press Confirm my wallet. MetaMask shows the shop, your name and your wallet. Sign only if that wallet is yours.
