The week, without the surprises

The mistakes that cost a shop money are small and easy to miss on a busy day. Embco looks at every bill before a single dollar leaves.

Billed for more than arrived

You ordered 4, the truck brought 3, the bill says 4. Embco compares the order, the delivery and the bill, and tells you what does not match.

A price that quietly went up

It knows what each supplier usually charges you. A jump stands out, and Embco tells you what changed.

The same bill, entered twice

Even when the number is typed a little differently. Only one gets paid.

"We changed our bank details"

The classic scam. New payment details are never paid until the supplier proves they are theirs and you approve them.

You stay in charge

An assistant that pays bills has to earn trust. Embco is built so it never has to ask for it blindly.

  • Your money never moves to us

    It stays in your own wallet on Arc. Each payment goes straight from you to your supplier.

  • Hard limits

    Per payment and per week. The agent cannot go over them, and cannot change them.

  • One switch to stop

    Turn it off from the app or from your phone. Payments stop at once.

  • Every payment has a receipt

    Recorded back in your books, with a public proof anyone can check.

  • Works alongside you

    Pay any bill yourself, the way you always have. Embco sees it is settled and moves on.

  • Paid in seconds

    In digital dollars (USDC, issued by Circle), settled on Arc. No bank cut-off times, no waiting days.

Questions

Do I need to know about crypto?

No. Payments go out in USDC, a digital dollar issued by Circle, from a wallet you control. The app walks you through the few clicks it takes.

Can I still pay my suppliers myself?

Yes, exactly as you do today. Embco works next to you: when you pay a bill yourself, it sees the bill is settled and leaves it alone.

What if it gets something wrong?

When something does not add up, it holds the bill and asks you. It can never pay more than your limits, and you can stop it at any time.

Does it change my books?

It only records the payments it makes, in the same place you would. Bills, orders and deliveries stay exactly as you entered them.

How do my suppliers get paid?

Straight to their wallet on Arc, in seconds, the moment the bill is approved. They can follow their bills and payments on their own page.

Is it live?

Embco runs today on Arc's test network with test dollars, so you can try every step without real money.
