04 Your cash, your rules

You stay in charge.
Your cash keeps working.

An assistant that pays bills has to earn trust. Embco is built so it never has to ask for it blindly.

Your wallet

Your money never moves to us

It stays in your own wallet on Arc. Each payment goes straight from you to your supplier, in seconds.

On chain

Hard limits

Per payment and per week, in your shop's contract. The agent cannot go over them, and cannot change them.

One switch

Stop it at once

Turn it off from the app or from Telegram on your phone. Payments stop at once.

Arc Earn

Idle cash earns while it waits

If you turn it on, the agent puts USDC no bill needs soon into a vault you approve, and takes it back when a bill needs it. The shares stay in your wallet.

Circle Gateway

Pays from the chains you allow

Short on Arc? If you keep USDC on Base or Ethereum, the agent brings what a bill needs to your wallet on Arc, with the fees estimated first.

Public receipt

Every payment can be checked

Recorded back in your books, and anchored on Arc, so anyone can check the agent's record was not changed.

05 Questions

Good questions. Plain answers.

Which ERP does it work with?

ERPNext, self-hosted or on Frappe Cloud. Embco reads the bills, orders and receipts you already keep there, and records each payment it makes back in it.

Do I need to know about crypto?

No. Payments go out in USDC, a digital dollar issued by Circle, from a wallet you control. The app walks you through the few clicks it takes.

Can I still pay my suppliers myself?

Yes, exactly as you do today. Embco works next to you: when you pay a bill yourself, it sees the bill is settled and leaves it alone.

What if it gets something wrong?

When something does not add up, it holds the bill and asks you. It can never pay more than your limits, and you can stop it at any time.

Is my money at risk to earn yield?

Only if you turn Earn on and approve a vault. The shares stay in your wallet, the agent deposits only within the limits you set, and it can only withdraw back to you. A vault lends to others, so it carries the risk of that lending: approve only the ones you trust.

Does it change my books?

It only records the payments it makes, in the same place you would. Bills, orders and deliveries stay exactly as you entered them.

How do my suppliers get paid?

Straight to their wallet on Arc, in seconds, the moment the bill is approved. They can follow their bills and payments on their own page.

Is it live?

Embco runs today on Arc's test network with test dollars, so you can try every step without real money. See what the agents have paid so far on the live stats page.
